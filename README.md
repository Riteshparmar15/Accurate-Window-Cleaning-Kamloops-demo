## 🍁 International & Canadian Standard Architecture

The website has been elevated to match the caliber of leading Canadian & international home-service brands (such as Shack Shine, Men In Kilts, Clearview, Gorilla Property Services) while remaining 100% original and customized for Kamloops, British Columbia.

### Key International & Canadian Features Built:

1. **Canadian Identity & Compliance Standards:**
   - **100% Canadian Owned & Operated** badge featuring the Canadian Maple Leaf crest.
   - **WorkSafeBC Registered** and in good standing with fall-arrest certification.
   - **$5,000,000 Commercial General Liability Insurance** badge.
   - **BBB Accredited A+ Business** trust signals.
   - All pricing displayed with transparent Canadian Dollar designations (`$ CAD`).

2. **Interactive Kamloops Postal Code Availability Checker:**
   - Positioned prominently in the Hero banner.
   - Instant validation for Greater Kamloops postal prefixes:
     - `V2C`: Downtown Kamloops, Valleyview, Juniper Ridge & Dallas
     - `V2E`: Sahali, Upper Sahali & Southgate
     - `V1S`: Aberdeen, Dufferin & Pineview
     - `V2B`: North Kamloops, Brocklehurst & Westsyde
     - `V2H`: Sun Rivers, Rayleigh & Heffley Creek
   - Auto-populates the quote address field so the customer doesn't have to retype.

3. **"How It Works" 3-Step Seamless Customer Journey:**
   - **Step 01:** 60-Second Instant Online Quote (No in-person home visit required).
   - **Step 02:** Eco-Pure Water Clean (0.00 PPM deionized water safe for BC lakes, pets, and plants).
   - **Step 03:** 100% Happiness Walk-Through & 7-Day Rain Guarantee.

4. **Transparent Residential Service Packages:**
   - **Essential Pure Water ($149 CAD):** Exterior Glass + Spot-Free Rinse + Sills Wipe.
   - **Deluxe In & Out ($229 CAD - Most Popular):** Full In/Out Glass + Deep Track Brush & Vacuum + Screen Pressure Wash + 7-Day Rain Protection.
   - **Full Property Revival Bundle ($389 CAD - Save $80+):** In & Out Windows + House Soft Wash Siding + Gutter Clearance & Downspout Flow Flush.

5. **Commercial, Strata & Property Management Portal:**
   - Dedicated section for Kamloops strata councils, real estate agents (pre-listing curb appeal), and commercial property managers.
   - Corporate Net-30 invoicing, WorkSafeBC clearance letters, and after-hours scheduling.

6. **Interactive Draggable Before-and-After Slider (CSS Clip-Path):**
   - 1:1 pixel-perfect alignment across mobile, tablet, and desktop.
   - Switchable categories: *Residential Windows*, *Driveway Pressure Washing*, and *Siding & Gutter Soft Wash*.

7. **Live Social Proof Activity Toast:**
   - Discreet floating notification in the bottom-left corner displaying realistic recent Kamloops bookings (Aberdeen, Sahali, Sun Rivers, Downtown) to drive conversion momentum.
