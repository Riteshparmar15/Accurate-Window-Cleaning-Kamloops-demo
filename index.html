Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our window cleaning, power washing, and scheduling in Kamloops.

We provide a 100% 7-Day Rain Guarantee. Pure deionized water leaves no detergent film for rain dust to cling to, so regular rain typically dries clean. However, if a sudden Kamloops storm leaves spots on your windows within 7 days, Kyle and the team will return and touch them up at no additional cost.
For exterior-only window cleaning, power washing, or gutter cleaning, you do not need to be home as long as outside water spigots are on, gates are unlocked, and pets are secured inside. We send text updates and photos when the job is completed! If we are doing interior glass, someone 18 or older should be present.
Yes, absolutely. Accurate Window Cleaning carries $5,000,000 in comprehensive commercial liability insurance and is fully registered in good standing with WorkSafeBC. We prioritize strict safety standards, specialized fall arrest systems, and water-fed carbon fiber poles for high-reach tasks.
We accept all standard Canadian payment options: Interac e-Transfer, Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and corporate invoicing for commercial properties. You only pay after the job is completed and inspected to your 100% satisfaction.
Our multi-stage system physically filters tap water through sediment filters, reverse osmosis membranes, and deionization resin until 0.00 parts-per-million of minerals remain. Because pure water acts like a natural solvent, no harsh soaps, detergents, or chemicals are washed into the Kamloops municipal storm drains or the Thompson River watershed.
